## 💡 Recommended Analytical Framework for your Project

### 1. Accessibility Metrics (Infrastructure Availability)
- **Sub-Centres (SCs), Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs)**:
  - Density of health centers per rural population.
  - Percentage of rural villages within 3km/5km of a functional PHC.
- **Physical Infrastructure Deficit**:
  - % of SCs/PHCs without regular electric supply, water supply, or all-weather roads.

### 2. Quality & Human Resource Deficits
- **Specialist Doctor Shortage**: Deficit % of Surgeons, Physicians, Obstetricians/Gynecologists, and Pediatricians at CHCs.
- **Facility Readiness**: % of PHCs functioning 24x7 with bed capacity and lab diagnostics.
- **Maternal & Child Healthcare Delivery**: Institutional delivery rates and immunization coverage in rural districts.
